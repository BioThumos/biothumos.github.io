Formulation · Curator assessment

The patient described this experimental nanocarrier formulation as intended to improve systemic or brain delivery, possibly via lymphatic uptake. Blood–brain barrier penetration and hepatic first-pass bypass were not independently measured; NC must not be treated as equivalent to every commercial liposome.
