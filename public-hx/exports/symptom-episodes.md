Retrospective patient and practitioner accounts; magnesium, disease fluctuation and exertion were also discussed. Timing does not prove NADH caused each symptom.

## Rapamycin and subsequent treatments · June–July 2025

Severe recurrent CNS/upper-body crashes followed a single rapamycin dose on June 21. The subsequent July treatment sequence also includes newly green stool and episodes of diarrhea. These gastrointestinal manifestations were reported later, after ensitrelvir started and around additional gut-directed products.

Rapamycin was not given repeatedly. Ensitrelvir began July 1, after the June neurological changes; green stool began July 2–6. Later diarrhea overlapped SuperHMO and Gou teng. These dates do not isolate any causal contributor.

### Recorded exposures in this period

- **Rapamycin** (Index exposure, 2025-06-21): One 0.5 mg dose preceded acute crashes and CNS/upper-body fatigue; causality is a patient suspicion. (intervention_events:S2-L00260-E2; context_cards:CTX-014)
- **Ensitrelvir** (Later exposure, 2025-07-01): Started ten days AFTER the rapamycin dose and after the acute CNS-fatigue onset; green stool first appeared July 2–6. Cannot explain the original June 21 crash. (intervention_events:S3:T:670:1; historical_entries:S3:chronology:671)
- **SuperHMO** (Later exposure, 2025-07-13): Later exposure during recurrent green/loose stool; exact first-day use and contribution are uncertain. (historical_entries:S3:chronology:675; intervention_events:S3:T:675:2)
- **Gou teng** (Later exposure, 2025-07-15): Started during later July gastrointestinal deterioration; independent effects cannot be separated from SuperHMO and prior symptoms. (intervention_events:S2-L00645-E1; historical_entries:S3:chronology:675)

- **Brain fatigue** — Possible onset after the episode. CNS and upper-body fatigue attacks became prominent around the single-dose trial and recurred subsequently. (pz_symptom_course_event:R20:history:cns_fatigue:onset:S3:treatment_response:212; context_cards:CTX-014)

- **Diarrhea** — Uncertain membership. Loose/diarrheal stools and worsening green diarrhea recurred later in July. ## Bezisterim and spore probiotic · November–December 2025

In the period encompassing the spore-probiotic trial and the December 1 bezisterim dose, the patient reported a new lactate-like quality of existing brain fatigue, a more prominent nape-fatigue presentation and wrist-specific fatigue. Facial/sinus pressure with behind-eye sensations was also described. The exposures overlapped and cannot be distinguished as the cause from these records.

Neither treatment is a confirmed cause. The spore probiotic began November 18 and was restarted November 26; bezisterim was taken December 1. Wrist-specific onset is retrospective; an earlier nape-fatigue episode is documented.
