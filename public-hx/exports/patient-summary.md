# Patient overview

Reviewed 2026-11-23

**Care setting:** The patient cannot tolerate travel for assessments following a substantial decline associated with the previous hospital admission. Care and monitoring are limited to the home; mobile phlebotomy is feasible.
- **Illness onset.** He reports about six weeks of acute illness after a positive COVID test in May 2023, followed by relapses. Symptoms became persistent in autumn 2023; by December he required family assistance.
- **October 2024 decline.** A major, sustained step-down in function developed during the October 2024 NADH exposure. The patient describes profound deep-muscle fatigue, severe physical PEM, marked weakness and confinement to bed, followed by extreme light, sound and medication intolerance. Earlier neurological PEM continued alongside the new severe symptom pattern. The temporal relationship with NADH is reported, not proof of causation.
- **Post-exertional malaise (PEM).** Minimal physical, cognitive, emotional or sensory exertion can provoke violent, profoundly disabling post-exertional malaise (PEM) or crashes. The patient describes painful, corrosive or poisoned sensations spreading through limbs, spine and central nervous system, forceful occipital pressure and nape clenching, profound brain fatigue and cognitive shutdown, and a wired state with internal tremors. Severe crashes may culminate in involuntary sleep and can persist or recur. Eating sometimes relieves milder episodes but does not reliably abort severe crashes; these are not ordinary fatigue.
- **Other recurring symptoms.** Severe cognitive dysfunction and brain fog, tinnitus and extreme light/sound intolerance coexist with severe orthostatic intolerance/POTS-type symptoms: standing for more than a few minutes markedly intensifies symptoms. There are also intermittent abdominal pain, constipation, diarrhea, reflux, gurgling and gas. Green stool began in July 2025 and was reported resolved by the November 2026 review.
- **Other major treatment-associated declines.** After a single 0.5 mg rapamycin dose on 21 June 2025, the patient reported approximately ten days of exceptionally violent, recurrent inflammatory/herx-like crashes—the worst he had experienced, subjectively so severe he felt he might die—with renewed brain burning/pressure and new severe CNS and upper-body fatigue. During overlapping late-2025 spore probiotic and bezisterim trials, he reported increased muscle fatigue and much stronger brain fatigue, followed by new forehead/eye symptoms and reduced screen tolerance. Individual attribution cannot be established from the overlapping exposures.
- **Function in November 2026.** Care is predominantly home-based; travel for assessment is not tolerated, although mobile blood draws are feasible. Prolonged conversation causes escalating severe, diffuse corrosive pain (historically described in some records as tingling), posterior nape clenching, CNS fatigue, a wired state and internal tremors; sufficiently prolonged exertion can trigger a crash the next day.
