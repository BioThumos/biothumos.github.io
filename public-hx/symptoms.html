Crash 201/817 days · 1.72/weekProfoundly disabling

Reported experience

A severe episode, qualitatively beyond ordinary PEM-like malaise, with abrupt major cognitive slowing and impaired ability to initiate movement, often profound systemic malaise and sometimes perceived hand/arm weakness. One form is a relatively "clean" Brain fatigue → sleep pressure → sleep crash, without prominent whole-body sensory symptoms; actual sleep is not obligatory. Another, often described as poisoned, involves intense painful internal tingling/pulsing in the brain and spine, sometimes severe internal hammering/tremors, and marked distress. In established crashes, forcing movement may briefly ease symptoms without ending the episode. Meals may relieve an early warning state but do not reliably arrest a full crash.

Reported experience

Selected medicines and supplements have been followed by unusually strong, sometimes rapid symptom flares: deep brainstem-like/nape pain or clenching, wrist/forearm fatigue, intensified internal tingling and sometimes a full PEM crash or sleep pressure. The response varies by substance, dose and background state. Examples include KL-1333, SkQ1 and methylene blue; liposomal P5P is only a possible association in a multi-supplement period. This is best understood as exposure-associated symptom exacerbation, not a universal reaction to all medications.
