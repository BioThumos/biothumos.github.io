Patient overview

Summary · Markdown
  • Onset. COVID-positive in May 2023; roughly six weeks of acute illness followed by relapses and persistent disease by autumn. Family assistance required by December.
  • Major decline · Oct 2024. During NADH exposure, profound muscle fatigue, disabling physical PEM and bed confinement developed, together with extreme light, sound and medication intolerance. A lasting major deterioration; NADH causation is not established.
  • PEM and crashes. Minimal physical, cognitive or sensory activity can provoke violent, incapacitating crashes with severe diffuse corrosive pain, nape clenching and occipital pressure, CNS fatigue, cognitive shutdown, internal tremors and wired arousal; some culminate in involuntary sleep. Eating may ease mild episodes but does not abort severe ones.
  • Other symptoms. Severe brain fog, tinnitus and light/sound intolerance; disabling orthostatic intolerance/POTS-type symptoms make standing beyond a few minutes intolerable. Abdominal pain, constipation or diarrhea; the 2025 green-stool phase resolved by late 2026.
  • Later deteriorations. A single 0.5 mg rapamycin dose (June 2025) preceded about ten days of extreme recurrent, inflammation-like crashes, described as the worst ever—severe enough that he felt he might die—with new CNS/upper-body fatigue and renewed brain burning. Late-2025 spore probiotic/bezisterim trials coincided with increased muscle and brain fatigue; attribution between exposures remains uncertain.
  • Current limitations. Home care necessary; travel for assessment not tolerated. Prolonged conversation exacerbates whole-body corrosive pain, nape clenching, CNS fatigue, internal tremors and wired arousal; longer conversations can cause a next-day crash. Mobile blood draws feasible.
