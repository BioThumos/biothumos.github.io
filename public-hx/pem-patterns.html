PEM-like state

Patient-described phases, qualities and independently tracked markers.

Possible progression — not inevitable

Vulnerable / crashy → PEM-like malaise → possible crash. The diary does not establish a fixed sequence, and a warning state may resolve without a severe attack. Reported worsening may follow exertion, sleep loss or treatment changes; individual causal triggers remain uncertain.

Separately tracked symptoms

The patient reports deep internal tingling/pulsing and light/noise intolerance at baseline with fluctuations and greater intensity during some PEM-like episodes. The recurring “evening PEM” tingling/pulsing pattern is tracked separately because it can occur without an exertional trigger.
