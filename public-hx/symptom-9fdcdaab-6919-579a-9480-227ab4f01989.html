Evening-type sensory waves / tingling

Calendar records · 96

Occurrence · Always present, fluctuating
Reported triggers · No consistent exertion trigger established; can recur on low-exertion days.

Basis
Patient-reported recurring background/rhythmic sensation historically called “evening PEM”; later described as evening pulsing or tingling. May start in the morning or afternoon despite its name. The patient describes it as near-daily; absence of an entry does not indicate absence of the sensation. A severe PEM crash or poisoned state may overlap but is not a prerequisite. The patient reports a similar return or intensification during the January 2026 S-acetyl-L-glutathione (SAG) trial; the SAG-like presentation could include stronger sinus/retro-orbital/forehead pressure, though sinus pressure started before SAG. An analogous presentation was described in April 2026 after a different treatment. Similarity does not establish a common mechanism or treatment causation.
