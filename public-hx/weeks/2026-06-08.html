Interpreting highlighted sleep stages

Deep sleep: under 45 min (red), 45–59 min (amber), at least 60 min (green). REM: under 15% (red), 15–19% or over 30% (amber), 20–30% (green). Core/light: 40–70% (green), 30–39% or 71–80% (amber), outside these (red). These are broad visual reference bands, not diagnostic thresholds. Age, total sleep time and Apple Watch stage-estimation error matter.
