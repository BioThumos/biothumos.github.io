Interpreting highlighted sleep stages

Deep sleep: under 45 min (red), 45–59 min (amber), at least 60 min (green). REM: under 15% (red), 15–19% or over 30% (amber), 20–30% (green). Age, total sleep time and Apple Watch stage-estimation error matter.
