Interpreting the measurements

Heart rate: intermittent Watch readings (beats/min). Daily values are medians of available samples, not 24-hour averages. Resting heart rate is Apple Health’s separately recorded estimate.

Heart-rate variability: Apple Health SDNN (ms), not RMSSD. Recording time, posture, breathing and activity can affect the values.

Wrist temperature: absolute overnight wrist skin temperature (°C) as exported; assigned to the morning the recording ended. It is not core body temperature and cannot establish fever.

Blood pressure: no systolic or diastolic readings were present in the supplied Apple Health export. Apple Watch does not directly measure blood pressure in this dataset.

Missing readings indicate missing data, not normal physiology. Months with different wear time or sampling are not directly comparable without accounting for coverage.

Measured sleep

Apple Watch sleep stages are present on 702 wake dates (2023-11-02–2025-12-07). Only asleep-stage intervals from the verified Watch-associated source were used; iPhone sleep schedules, “in bed” intervals and unverified sources were excluded. Where the diary and Watch overlap, measured Watch sleep stages generally take priority. Two Watch sleep periods were corrected using timed diary observations. Subjective sleep quality remains available from the diary. A missing Watch night does not indicate zero sleep.
