# Tweet by Zedzies

> Age associated dips in ATP production, mitochondrial membrane potential or membrane physiology all creates vicious cycle that fuel each other
> 
> Bad membrane physiology (low CL, stiffer membranes, lipid peroxidation) creates an environment where oxPhos cannot run efficiently thus lowering MMP
> 
> This lowering is accompanied by a drop in ATP which is needed to fuel the translocases within the membranes, which transport lipids from the inner or outer leaflet
> 
> Interestingly, scramblases are such translocases that do not need ATP. They are often activated by Ca and under apoptotic conditions. Proper membrane physiology is protective of such processes by lowering the threshold of apoptosis and also influencing Ca/MMTP homeoastasis
> 
> Exogenous supplementation of phospholipids (best if mixed, liposomal and attached to FOS) in the range of 1,5-3g have been shown in human studies to improve such conditions
> 
> This is just one layer to lipid replacement
