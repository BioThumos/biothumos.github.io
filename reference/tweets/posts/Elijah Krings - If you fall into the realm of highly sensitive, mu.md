> If you fall into the realm of highly sensitive, multi complex patient in the ME/CFS sphere:
> 
> After hearing many podcasts, reading a lot of case studies and also having clients falling into that camp as well I think one of the earliest interventions should be
> 
> Lipid Replacement Therapy
> 
> It often is tolerated *pretty well* if done gently and it provides so many broad spectrum effects over a longer time horizon. To make this quick:
> 
> It improves detoxification of membrane bound toxins like mycotoxins
> 
> By improving mitochondrial membrane make up you can restore oxPhos function and therefore energy metabolism
> 
> By improving the above, you necessarily will improve upon inflammatory states and also be more resilient.
> 
> As you will increase your intake of vitamin e, astaxanthin, plasmalogens and hopefully switch to a high omega-3 diet you will be less prone to inflammatory overshoots
> 
> You gently restore how cells function, transfer and receive information via cytokines, hormones and other mediators
> 
> The basic stack could look something like:
> 
> - omega-3 rich diet (fish 3-4x/week + high dosage EPA/DHA until omega-3s are in check). this provides DHA/EPA, selenium, zinc, astaxanthin and iodine
> - ditch seed oils from low quality sources (certain seeds, olive oil and egg yolks as sources)
> - provide plenty of choline through eggs & liver
> 
> Then from a supplement standpoint you might look at:
> 
> - Astaxanthin
> - n3/n9 plasmalogen therapy
> - High dosage phospholipids
> - Olive Leaf Extract (Hydroxy Tyrosol)
> - Vitamin E (gamma-tocopherols as well!)
